PROPOSED SOLUTION
Idea Overview, Root-Cause Problem Resolution & Key Innovations
Slide 2 of 6
1. Solution Description
  • Real-Time Vigilance Platform: Replaces slow historical ledgers with automated fraud prevention before funds are disbursed.
  • 6 Independent Signals: Evaluates every project proposal across pricing, networks, guidelines, and mathematics.
  • Explain-My-Score Waterfall: Scores 0 to 100 with itemized additive points citing exact GFR 2017 clauses.
2. Problems Addressed
  • Double-Invoicing: Compares road/hall works with ISRO Bhuvan & MGNREGA to catch cross-scheme billing.
  • Inflated Estimates: Benchmarks costs against CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR) to flag overpricing.
  • Vendor Cartels: Network graph uncovers single-agency fund monopolies (70%+ budget share).
  • Zombie Projects: Survival hazard models predict stalled projects 6 months before deadlines lapse.
3. Innovation & Uniqueness
  • 100% Real Government Data: Validated on 176,925 works and 109,521 payment vouchers from live MoSPI endpoints.
  • Data Provenance Framework: Every number carries an immutable badge (REAL, DERIVED, ESTIMATED, STATUTORY). Zero black boxes.
  • 1-Click Inspection Dossier: Pre-filled official memorandum for District Engineers to verify physical assets.
💡 The Core Differentiator: While other systems report what happened years ago, SATARK equips administrators with audit-defensible proof grounded in GFR 2017 rules before public money is lost.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
Scale Validation, Potential Risks & Strategic Mitigations
Slide 4 of 6
1. Feasibility at Government Scale
  • Demonstrated Nationwide Scale: Successfully indexed and analyzed 100% of India (36 States & UTs, 541 Constituencies, 176,925 works) in 1.8 seconds.
  • Zero Costly Infrastructure: Operates on standard lightweight server infrastructure without expensive cloud GPU clusters or paid third-party APIs.
  • Seamless Adoption: Connects directly to existing MoSPI eSAKSHI data pipelines without requiring workflow replacement.
2. Challenges & Strategic Mitigations
  • Challenge 1: Scale Risk (27,000 Graph Nodes Freezing UI)
    Mitigation: Focus & Context pattern renders only 1-2 hop neighborhood (10-15 nodes) on demand instead of the full national graph.
  • Challenge 2: Changing Statutory Rules (GFR / MPLADS Revisions)
    Mitigation: Policy-as-Data architecture stores rules with effective date ranges; past flags remain audit-defensible years later.
  • Challenge 3: Cross-Department Name Discrepancies
    Mitigation: Hierarchical blocking (State + District block) followed by n-gram token similarity eliminates false matches.
⚙️ Viability Guarantee: Every architectural decision maps directly to a recognized failure mode in public sector software deployments: scale lag, guideline drift, and cross-department data noise.
