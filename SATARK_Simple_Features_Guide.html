MPLADS-SATARK (सतर्क) — Simple Feature Guide

An Easy, Plain-Language Explanation of All 7 AI Detectives to Share with Friends & Team
Problem Statement
PS 26102 (MoSPI / DIID)
Project Title
MPLADS-SATARK (Automated Forensic Vigilance)
Dataset Scale
176,925 Real Government Projects | 37 States & UTs
Core Purpose
Pre-Disbursement Audit & Multi-Layer Fraud Prevention
1. VIDHI-KAVACH (विधि-कवच)
The Legal Shield & Rules Referee
Sentinel 1 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Checks if government laws, SC/ST mandatory quotas, and MPLADS guidelines are strictly followed before money is disbursed.
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
Think of this like a referee in a football match. Government money has strict rules — for example, you cannot use public funds to build private clubhouses, luxury guest houses, or buy private cars. It also stops 'March Rush' (when officers try to spend all remaining budget in the last few days of March just so the funds don't expire).
⚡ Real-World Example: If someone tries to pass a bill for an unapproved private building in mid-March, VIDHI-KAVACH immediately blows the whistle and blocks it!
2. PUNAR-DRISHTI (पुनर्दृष्टि)
The Double-Dipping & Clone Sentry
Sentinel 2 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Catches duplicate or copy-pasted projects using smart text-matching (TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity).
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
Imagine a dishonest contractor builds one concrete road in a village using rural scheme money (like MGNREGA). Then, he submits the exact same road under MPLADS scheme funds to get paid twice for the same work! PUNAR-DRISHTI compares project titles across scheme databases.
⚡ Real-World Example: It compares 'PCC Road near GP Office' in both databases and catches double-billing instantly!
3. ARTHA-DARPAN (अर्थ-दर्पण)
The Price Inflation & Rate Book Checker
Sentinel 3 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Compares claimed project costs against standard government benchmarks (CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates).
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
Imagine going to buy a phone that costs ₹80,000, but the shopkeeper charges you ₹3,00,000. ARTHA-DARPAN keeps a standard price book for every state in India. If a standard 500-meter road usually costs ₹5 Lakhs, but a bill comes in for ₹18 Lakhs, ARTHA-DARPAN flags it.
⚡ Real-World Example: It flags excess expenditure and calculates exact overpricing (e.g., '₹13 Lakhs excess risk flagged').
4. CHAKRA-VYUH (चक्रव्यूह)
The Contractor Syndicate & Cartel Hunter
Sentinel 4 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Exposes hidden networks and cartels between contractors, agencies, and officers using network graph theory.
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
Sometimes, 3 or 4 contractor companies pretend to compete against each other in open tenders, but behind closed doors, they are all owned by the same family or group! CHAKRA-VYUH builds a visual web (social network graph) connecting MPs, agencies, and vendors to see who is getting all the contracts.
⚡ Real-World Example: If one single contractor secretly captures 85% of all project contracts in a district, CHAKRA-VYUH flags a Monopoly/Cartel Risk.
5. VIBHED-NETRA (विभेद-नेत्र)
The 12D AI Anomaly Spotter
Sentinel 5 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Finds suspicious projects by evaluating 12 different clues simultaneously using Machine Learning (Isolation Forest).
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
A human auditor can only look at 1 or 2 details at a time (like cost or date). But this AI looks at 12 details simultaneously (cost, duration, completion rate, location, payment pattern, etc.) to spot weird outliers.
⚡ Real-World Example: A project that was approved in 1 day, spent 100% of its budget on day 2, but has zero physical progress stands out like a sore thumb to the AI.
6. SANKHYA-SATYA (संख्या-सत्य)
The Fake Bill & Tender-Split Finder
Sentinel 6 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Uses financial math (Benford's Law) and pattern recognition to catch fabricated numbers and tender splitting.
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
1) Tender-Splitting: Rules say any project above ₹5 Lakhs MUST go through an open public tender. Dishonest people split a ₹14 Lakh project into three fake bills of ₹4.95 Lakhs each to bypass tenders! SANKHYA-SATYA catches this trick. 2) Benford's Law: In real financial records, the number 1 appears as the first digit about 30% of the time. When humans make up fake bills from thin air, they overuse round numbers (like ₹5,00,000). Math catches them!
⚡ Real-World Example: Flags artificial bill splitting (smurfing) and round-number human-fabricated estimates.
7. BHU-DRISHTI (भू-दृष्टि)
The Satellite & Ghost Asset Radar
Sentinel 7 of 7
🔍 What it does:
Uses GPS coordinates and ISRO satellite maps to check if the project actually exists on the ground.
💡 How to explain it to a friend:
What if someone claims ₹15 Lakhs for a 'Community Center', but it was never built? BHU-DRISHTI checks the exact GPS latitude and longitude on satellite maps.
⚡ Real-World Example: If the GPS location shows empty barren land, a lake, or a forest where a building was claimed to be constructed, BHU-DRISHTI flags it as a Ghost Asset!
🎯 Unified Priority Score & Official Vigilance Memo
Master Risk Aggregator & Legal Memo Sentry
📊 0 to 100 Composite Risk Score:
Combines all 7 sentinels into one clear score from 0 to 100. 0-30 is Green (Clean), 30-70 is Yellow (Medium Risk), and 70-100 is Red (Critical Fraud Risk).
📜 1-Click Legal Memorandum:
With one click, auditors can generate and print an official legal vigilance memorandum (Form GFR-19A) with Government letterhead, rule citations, and a field verification checklist!
