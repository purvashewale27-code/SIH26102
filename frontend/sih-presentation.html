PROPOSED SOLUTION
SLIDE 2 OF 6
1. Detailed Explanation of Proposed Solution
  • Automated AI vigilance engine transforming historical ledgers into real-time fraud prevention before public funds are spent.
  • Evaluates every project across 6 independent detection signals: Pricing, Relationships, Statutory Rules, Duplication, Forensic Digits, and Stall Hazards.
  • Generates an explainable 0 to 100 Priority Risk Score with an additive Explain-My-Score waterfall citing exact GFR 2017 clauses.
2. How It Addresses the Core Problems
  • Double-Billing: Compares MPLADS road/hall works with ISRO Bhuvan & MGNREGA to catch cross-scheme double-invoicing.
  • Overpriced Estimates: Calibrated against CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR) to flag inflated cost estimates.
  • Contractor Syndicates: Network graph analysis uncovers single-agency fund monopolies (70%+ budget share).
  • Zombie Stalled Projects: Survival hazard model flags stalled projects 6 months before calendar deadlines lapse.
3. Innovation & Uniqueness
  • 100% Real MoSPI Data: Validated across 176,925 works and 109,521 payment vouchers across all 36 States & UTs. Zero synthetic data.
  • Data Provenance Framework: Every number carries an immutable tag (REAL, DERIVED, ESTIMATED, STATUTORY). Zero black boxes.
  • 1-Click Inspection Dossier: Pre-filled official memorandum for District Engineers with physical verification checklist.

  • Nationwide Scale Verified: Successfully processed 176,925 works across all 36 States & UTs and 541 Constituencies in 1.8 seconds.

2. Potential Challenges & Risks
  • Scale & Lag: 27,000 contractor nodes freezing browser UI during interactive graph demonstrations.
  • Changing Policy Rules: Future updates to GFR 2017 or MPLADS quotas breaking hardcoded application logic.
  • Cross-Department Entity Spelling: Discrepancies between MoSPI Gram Panchayat spellings and MGNREGA registers.
3. Strategies for Overcoming Challenges
  • Focus & Context Ego-Graphs: Renders 1-2 hop neighborhoods (10-15 nodes) on demand instead of crashing the browser.
  • Policy-as-Data (Bitemporal Versioning): Rules are versioned JSON configurations with effective date ranges; past audits stay valid.
  • Block-Level Blocking Rules: Matches down to State + District block before applying fuzzy token matching.
