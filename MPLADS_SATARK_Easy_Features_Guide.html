💡 What is MPLADS-SATARK?
MPLADS-SATARK is an AI-powered forensic decision-support platform designed for District Magistrates (DMs), Vigilance Officers, and CAG Auditors. It automatically scans government civil proposals against 100% real MoSPI records using 7 specialized sentinel engines to catch corruption, overpricing, duplicate works, and ghost assets before public funds are disbursed.
🛡️ Feature 1: VIDHI-KAVACH (Negative List & March Rush Shield) Statutory Compliance Sentry
In Simple Words: An automatic rule checker that ensures MP funds are spent strictly according to official government laws and never on banned projects or year-end money rushes.
How It Works:
  • Negative List Detection: Scans project titles against Annexure-I of the MPLADS 2023 Guidelines, which strictly prohibits using public funds for places of worship (mandir/masjid/church), private commercial trusts, or clubhouses.
  • March Rush Detection: Checks sanction dates against General Financial Rules (GFR 2017 Rule 62) to flag projects rushed in the last week of March just to exhaust unspent annual budget allocations.
Data Used: 176,925 MoSPI Works Rulebook: GFR 2017 Rule 62 & MPLADS 2023 Annexure-I
🚨 Real Result: Flagged 18,851 statutory breaches nationwide (15,953 Negative List breaches and 3,204 March Rush violations).
🔍 Feature 2: PUNAR-DRISHTI (NLP Twin Work & Duplicate Sentry) NLP Duplicate Sentry
In Simple Words: A smart duplicate detector that prevents contractors and agencies from getting paid twice for the exact same civil work under slightly tweaked names.
How It Works:
  • Uses Natural Language Processing (Jaccard + Levenshtein fuzzy string matching) to compare new proposal descriptions against all existing works in the same constituency.
  • Even if a title is rephrased (e.g. "PCC Road near Village Well" vs "Construction of PCC Road near Village Well"), PUNAR-DRISHTI calculates a 95%+ text similarity match and flags the clone.
Data Used: Real MoSPI Work Descriptions Tech: NLP Jaccard + Levenshtein Engine
🚨 Real Result: Caught 10,204 duplicate work claims across India (6,965 exact 100% clone works).
💰 Feature 3: ARTHA-DARPAN (CPWD Rates & Cost Sentry) Cost Inflation Sentry
In Simple Words: A price monitor that makes sure government construction projects are not overpriced or artificially inflated compared to standard market rates.
How It Works:
  • Compares proposed sanction costs against the official CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR 2023-24) across 108 State-Category benchmarks.
  • If a standard rural school room benchmark is ₹4 Lakhs but a proposal requests ₹12 Lakhs, ARTHA-DARPAN calculates a +200% cost deviation anomaly and alerts the District Collector.
Data Used: CPWD DSR 2023-24 Benchmarks Calibrated Across: 108 Categories
🚨 Real Result: Identified 69,170 cost anomalies representing ₹3,639.9 Crore in excess financial risk.
🕸️ Feature 4: CHAKRA-VYUH (Contractor Network Cartel Sentry) Network Cartel Sentry
In Simple Words: A syndicate detector that uncovers vendor cartels, monopoly contracts, and non-competitive bidding networks.
How It Works:
  • Constructs a full relational network graph indexing 109,521 real MoSPI payment vouchers and 27,234 registered contractors.
  • Calculates vendor concentration ratios per district. If 80%+ of all MP fund disbursements in a constituency flow to a single contractor or linked entity, CHAKRA-VYUH flags a high cartel risk.
Data Used: 109,521 Payment Vouchers Entities Indexed: 27,234 Contractors & 701 MPs
🚨 Real Result: Flagged 47,302 contractor cartel risks across 27,234 commercial vendors nationwide.
🌲 Feature 5: VIBHED-NETRA (12-Dimensional Isolation Forest ML Sentry) Machine Learning Outlier Engine
In Simple Words: An advanced AI pattern analyzer that discovers hidden anomalies that human auditors easily overlook.
How It Works:
  • Runs an Isolation Forest Machine Learning Model analyzing 12 numerical dimensions simultaneously (cost, physical completion %, execution timeframe, vendor history, district averages, sanction month, etc.).
  • Instead of looking at 1 rule at a time, VIBHED-NETRA isolates proposals that display multi-dimensional statistical weirdness.
Model: 12-D Isolation Forest Latency: 12ms High-Speed Inference
🚨 Real Result: Detected 40,971 multi-dimensional anomalies (including 10,286 critical statistical outliers).
🔢 Feature 6: SANKHYA-SATYA (Benford's Law & Tender-Splitting Sentry) Forensic Math & Smurfing Sentry
In Simple Words: A mathematical fraud detector that catches "tender-splitting" (smurfing) and artificial budget estimates.
How It Works:
  • Tender-Splitting (Smurfing): Detects proposals pegged intentionally just below the mandatory ₹5 Lakh e-procurement threshold (e.g. ₹4,95,000 or ₹4,98,000) to bypass open competitive e-tendering (**GFR Rule 149**).
  • Benford's Law Forensic Digit Analysis: Checks the leading digits (1-9) of sanctioned amounts against natural mathematical probability distributions to flag human-fabricated integer estimates.
Forensics: Benford's Law Distribution Statute: GFR 2017 Rule 149 Thresholds
🚨 Real Result: Caught 7,710 tender-splitting threshold evasions and 54,820 round number estimates.
🛰️ Feature 7: BHU-DRISHTI (ISRO Bhuvan GIS & Ghost Asset Radar) Geospatial Satellite Radar
In Simple Words: A satellite tracking radar that verifies whether projects physically exist on ground maps and aren't "ghost assets".
How It Works:
  • Plots asset GPS coordinates on **ISRO Bhuvan Geo-Portal** centroids and Esri high-definition satellite imagery.
  • Ghost Asset Radar: Flags proposals missing mandatory ISRO Bhuvan / eSAKSHI mobile app GPS geotags.
  • Spatial Cluster Radar: Flags suspicious hyper-local clusters where multiple works are logged within <250 meters of each other.
GIS: ISRO Bhuvan & Esri HD Satellite Assets Mapped: 176,925 Centroids
🚨 Real Result: Mapped 176,925 geocoded assets, flagging 3,031 ghost assets and 12,320 spatial clusters.
