SATARK — Complete Feature Overview

Automated AI Risk Scoring, Forensic Audit & Multi-Layer Anomaly Detection Engine (PS 26102)
Problem Statement
PS 26102 (MoSPI / DIID)
Ministry
Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation
Dataset Scale
176,925 Real Government Projects | 37 States & UTs
Total Modules
7 AI Sentinels + 6 Core Architecture Pillars (13 Modules)
🛡️ Part 1: The 7 Independent AI Sentinels
VIDHI-KAVACH (विधि-कवच)
Purpose: Legal & Rule-Based Fraud Detection
SENTINEL 1
⚙️ Technology: Indic Lexicon + Rule Matrix
📌 What it does:
Checks whether a proposed MPLADS work violates government rules or eligibility conditions.
🔬 How it works:
  • Reads the proposed work description.
  • Identifies keywords such as temples, trusts, prohibited activities, etc.
  • Compares the proposal against predefined MPLADS rules.
  • Checks suspicious budget/timing patterns such as March Rush.
🚨 Detects:
Ineligible/prohibited works and statutory rule violations.
📊 Output:
Rule violation / compliance flag with the exact reason.
🎯 Key Benefit: Prevents funds from being sanctioned for legally or procedurally invalid works.
PUNAR-DRISHTI (पुनर्दृष्टि)
Purpose: Duplicate Work Detection
SENTINEL 2
⚙️ Technology: TF-IDF + Cosine Similarity
📌 What it does:
Identifies the same or highly similar work being submitted more than once across schemes.
🔬 How it works:
  • Converts work descriptions into numerical representations.
  • Compares new proposals with existing proposals across databases.
  • Measures textual similarity even when the title has been changed or rephrased.
  • Example: 'Construction of Community Hall' vs 'Development of Village Multipurpose Hall'.
🚨 Detects:
Duplicate or suspiciously similar proposals.
📊 Output:
Similarity score percentage (e.g. 92% match) and matched project ID.
🎯 Key Benefit: Helps prevent duplicate funding and repeated submission of the same work.
ARTHA-DARPAN (अर्थ-दर्पण)
Purpose: Cost & Price Manipulation Detection
SENTINEL 3
⚙️ Technology: CPWD DSR Rates + Per-Unit Median Analysis
📌 What it does:
Checks whether the estimated project cost appears unusually high compared to government benchmarks.
🔬 How it works:
  • Takes project quantities and estimated costs.
  • Compares rates against CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR 2023-24) benchmarks.
  • Calculates expected/typical cost ranges for all 37 States/UTs.
  • Flags significant upward cost deviations.
🚨 Detects:
Overpricing, inflated estimates, price padding, and abnormally high unit rates.
📊 Output:
Cost anomaly flag & excess risk score in INR ₹.
🎯 Key Benefit: Helps identify potential financial irregularities before sanction.
CHAKRA-VYUH (चक्रव्यूह)
Purpose: Contractor/Vendor Network Analysis
SENTINEL 4
⚙️ Technology: Graph Theory + Ego-Graphs (Vis.js / Louvain)
📌 What it does:
Finds suspicious relationships between contractors, vendors, agencies, and projects.
🔬 How it works:
  • Represents contractors, vendors, and projects as an interconnected network.
  • Creates connections based on common contracts, locations, and officials.
  • Looks for highly connected or repeatedly appearing entities using Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI).
🚨 Detects:
Contractor concentration, vendor monopolies, cartel-like networks, and repeated contractor-project relationships.
📊 Output:
Graph centrality score & cartel risk alert.
🎯 Key Benefit: Reveals hidden syndicate patterns that are difficult to identify by checking individual records.
BHU-DRISHTI (भू-दृष्टि)
Purpose: Geospatial & Physical Verification
SENTINEL 5
⚙️ Technology: GIS + Remote Sensing + Geotagging (Leaflet / Esri Satellite)
📌 What it does:
Checks whether a sanctioned asset/work actually exists at the claimed geographic location.
🔬 How it works:
  • Uses project coordinates and GPS geotags.
  • Maps the project location using GIS satellite layers.
  • Compares available geographic and satellite evidence.
  • Identifies suspicious or unverifiable locations (water bodies, forests, barren land).
🚨 Detects:
Ghost assets, incorrect coordinates, location mismatches, and non-existent works.
📊 Output:
GIS Spatial anomaly flag & spatial cluster alert (<250m).
🎯 Key Benefit: Adds a physical verification layer to digital records.
SANKHYA-SATYA (संख्या-सत्य)
Purpose: Tender & Financial Pattern Detection
SENTINEL 6
⚙️ Technology: Benford's Law + Smurfing Rules
📌 What it does:
Looks for suspicious tender patterns that indicate attempts to avoid open public scrutiny.
🔬 How it works:
  • Analyzes financial amounts and transaction patterns.
  • Detects unusual numerical distributions on leading digits (1-9).
  • Looks for multiple smaller transactions/works that collectively represent a larger suspicious amount.
🚨 Detects:
Tender splitting (e.g. ₹4.95L bills), artificially small project values, suspicious financial distributions, and threshold-avoidance.
📊 Output:
Benford Chi-Square goodness-of-fit score & tender-split alert.
🎯 Key Benefit: Helps identify financial patterns that may not be obvious from individual transactions.
VIBHED-NETRA (विभेद-नेत्र)
Purpose: Advanced Multi-Parameter Anomaly Detection
SENTINEL 7
⚙️ Technology: 12D Isolation Forest Machine Learning
📌 What it does:
Detects complex anomalies by considering multiple project parameters simultaneously.
🔬 How it works:
  • Takes 12 dimensions: cost, date, location, vendor, project type, execution time, and administrative parameters.
  • Machine learning identifies non-linear combinations that look unusual compared with normal projects.
🚨 Detects:
Complex multi-dimensional anomalies that individual rule-based checks may miss.
📊 Output:
Multi-variate anomaly score & outlier severity rating.
🎯 Key Benefit: Provides an additional AI/ML-based fraud detection layer.
🧠 Part 2: Core SATARK Architecture (6 Modules)
SATARK-DRISHTI (सतर्क-दृष्टि)
Purpose: Unified Risk Scoring
Module 8 of 13
⚙️ Technology: Composite Risk Scorer (0–100)
📌 What it does:
Combines the results of all 7 Sentinel engines into a single dashboard risk score.
🔬 How it works:
  • Collects alerts from all 7 Sentinels.
  • Assigns weights and severity to different risks.
  • Produces a single overall composite risk score (0-30 Low, 31-60 Moderate, 61-80 High, 81-100 Critical).
  • Estimates potential project delay and risk trends.
🎯 Key Benefit: Gives officers a single dashboard-level risk assessment instead of checking seven systems separately.
SATARK-SAMVAAD (सतर्क-संवाद)
Purpose: AI-Powered Government Data Assistant
Module 9 of 13
⚙️ Technology: GenAI Natural Language Copilot
📌 What it does:
Allows officers to ask questions about government and project records in plain language.
🔬 How it works:
  • Officer asks a normal-language question in English or Hindi.
  • AI interprets the intent, location, budget, and sentinel filter.
  • Searches 176,925 MoSPI government records in real time.
  • Returns a concise answer with supporting evidence table.
🎯 Key Benefit: Makes large government datasets easier for non-technical users to analyze.
PRASHNA-KAVACH (प्रश्न-कवच)
Purpose: Explainable AI / Reason Behind the Alert
Module 10 of 13
⚙️ Technology: SHAP Explainable AI Drawer
📌 What it does:
Explains why SATARK flagged a project with transparent evidence attribution.
🔬 How it works:
  • Breaks down factors contributing to a risk score.
  • Shows which variables had the strongest influence.
  • Example: High cost deviation (+25), Suspicious vendor network (+20), Location anomaly (+15).
  • Answers: 'Why was this project flagged?'
🎯 Key Benefit: Makes AI decisions transparent and easier for auditors and officers to trust and verify.
SATARK-SIMULATION (सतर्क-सिम्युलेशन)
Purpose: Pre-Sanction What-If Testing
Module 11 of 13
⚙️ Technology: Real-Time Sub-15ms Sandbox Environment
📌 What it does:
Allows officials to test changes to a proposal in <15ms before approving it.
🔬 How it works:
  • Creates a simulated copy of the proposal.
  • Officer changes parameters such as cost, vendor, or project details.
  • SATARK immediately recalculates the risk across all 7 sentinels.
  • Example: ₹50 Lakh estimate -> ₹40 Lakh estimate -> risk score recalculates in 2ms.
🎯 Key Benefit: Supports better decisions before funds are sanctioned.
BHAVISHYA-REKHA (भविष्य-रेखा)
Purpose: Future Risk & Trend Forecasting
Module 12 of 13
⚙️ Technology: Linear Regression & Time-Series Forecasting
📌 What it does:
Predicts potential future risk trends and March Rush surges from historical project data.
🔬 How it works:
  • Analyzes historical project patterns.
  • Identifies trends such as increasing March-Rush activity.
  • Forecasts future quarterly risk patterns.
  • Highlights seasonal risk spikes and high-risk periods.
🎯 Key Benefit: Moves SATARK from reactive detection to proactive prevention.
SATARK-KARYA (सतर्क-कार्य)
Purpose: Automated Vigilance & Documentation
Module 13 of 13
⚙️ Technology: GFR-19A Memo Builder + SHA-256 Hashing
📌 What it does:
Converts detected violations into formal legal documentation for further administrative action.
🔬 How it works:
  • Takes investigation and alert details.
  • Generates a structured Form GFR-19A vigilance memo/report.
  • Includes supporting evidence and 5-point engineer inspection checklist.
  • Uses SHA-256 hashing for document integrity.
🎯 Key Benefit: Reduces manual paperwork and creates a consistent audit and action trail.
