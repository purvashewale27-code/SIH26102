Proposed Solution & Core Innovation
1. Detailed Solution Overview:

  • Statutory Ground Actionability: Automatically generates an official printable Form GFR-19A Vigilance Memorandum with MoSPI emblem, barcode, legal citations, and physical engineer verification checklists.
3. Key Innovations & Uniqueness:
  • Zero "Black-Box" AI (Additive Waterfall Scoring): Unified composite risk score (0–100) breaks down into transparent legal rule deductions and GFR citations.
  • Cryptographic Dataset Integrity: Immutable SHA-256 provenance hash (9a5c8df1b038c352...) proving zero synthetic data fabrication.
  • Statutory HITL Protocol Charter: Respects constitutional norms—no public funds or vendor accounts are frozen autonomously without DM signoff.
